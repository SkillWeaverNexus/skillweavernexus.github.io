Crafting

The enterprising adventurer may often wish to take fate into their own hands by crafting useful supplies, armor, weapons, gadgets, potions, and numerous other useful items for use in their travels.

Many of the origin abilities, and several tree abilities, reference and make use of certain crafting guidelines as laid out in this chapter. Generally, crafting is considered a Downtime Activity, and follows the project structure for Downtime. However, some items can be crafted in shorter amounts of time, as specified by the relevant ability.

Basic Principles¶

All forms of crafting require some form of input materials. These materials are tagged by their rarity in four main categories, with some examples that might apply to, say, a tinkerer/engineer.

  • Common: Basic materials available in most settlements (wood, common metals, simple tools)
  • Uncommon: Specialized materials found in cities (quality metals, precision parts, basic crystals)
  • Rare: Exotic materials requiring special sources (special alloys, precision components, refined crystals)
  • Very Rare: Exceptional materials from specific sources (adamantine, perfect components)

The output from crafting is defined by a quality scale; the scale of the item being crafted defines the rough DL required to craft it, with the specific value set by your GM:

  • Common: Jury Rigged or temporary devices (DL 15)
  • Simple: Basic mechanical function, few moving parts (DL 20)
  • Moderate: Several moving parts, requires precision (DL 25)
  • Complex: Multiple integrated systems, fine tolerances (DL 30)
  • Masterwork: Cutting-edge design, extremely intricate (DL 50)

The above examples are again for a tinkered device, but similar tags apply to potions/elixirs, alchemical solutions, weapons, armor, and the like.

Crafting Process¶

Once you’ve gathered the components needed to craft an item and have sufficient time to do so, you make checks as specified by the item type, toward their completion. For downtime projects, checks take up time, and generally only 2 can be made per downtime. For short 10 minute or 1 hour builds, you have a single check to make the DL. A success creates a functioning device; failure might result in a flawed device or wasted materials

Crafted items will define whether they are single use (like a dose of a potion) or durable (like a prosthetic). If you spend 1 project in downtime maintaining your crafted devices, they will work until your next downtime. If your maintenance lapses (or other things are more important), your devices are subject to break if you roll a critical failure when using them.

If your GM supports it, you can work together to invent or develop new craftables beyond the ones provided in this list. It is up to the GM to determine the DL, material cost, and output complexity of such items.
