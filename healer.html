Healer

You have medical training and are proficient in diagnosing and healing living things, using a mix of mundane and magical means. You have major advantage in identifying physical ailments, including the effects of diseases, poisons, venom, and bodily injury (broken bones, pulled muscles). When you successfully identify or diagnose such an ailment, you generally know the required path to healing or curing the effect. If you can cast magic, your Arcanic die is Nature.

Features¶

Combat Medic

You are proficient in the use of a Medicine Kit, which you can use to provide nonmagical healing to your companions. Furthermore, you add a d6 to the standard healing from the kit when you use it.

Healing Aura

You exude healing energy. Allies that start their turn within 5 feet of you gain 1d4 temporary hit points.

Enhanced Healing

When you cast healing spells that involve multiple dice, you can reroll the lowest rolled die and keep the higher value.

Backstory Hooks
  1. Where did you train your medical skills and learn your magic (if you have magic)?
  2. What led you to pursue becoming a healer?
  3. Do you feel conflicted about using offensive weaponry or hurting others? If not, how do you square the healing and harming part of your life?
