# Physicality 

Physicality governs all forms of physical exertion, whether the precision of balance, the brute force of raw strength, or the endurance to keep going when others falter. It is invoked any time the body is the primary driver of success.

## Acrobatics
Acrobatics captures the fine-tuned control of one's body --- used for balancing on narrow ledges, flipping through tight spaces, or generally using the body with precision and flair. For example, 

- Walking along a tightrope or slippery beam.
- Hopping from safe stone to safe stone in a trapped hallway.
- Jumping from a rooftop and landing on a narrow balcony.


## Strength 
The raw power of muscles, Strength is used for direct feats of brawn that require exerting force, often with little concern for finesse.

- Prying open rusted metal bars.
- Lifting a fallen log off a trapped ally.
- Holding a portcullis open for your party to escape.


## Endurance
Endurance reflects the ability to persist over time --- to resist fatigue, carry weight over distance, or keep performing in the face of exhaustion.

- Running for miles across difficult terrain.
- Holding onto a rope or ledge for a prolonged period.
- Resisting collapse in a freezing blizzard.


## Sprint
Sprint is the measure of short-term, explosive physical action. It is used when sudden bursts of energy are needed.

- Dashing to catch a fleeing suspect.
- Escaping an explosion or cave-in.
- Lunging to intercept a flying object.


## Climb
Climb governs your ability to scale surfaces, whether natural or artificial, sheer or jagged.

- Scaling the wall of a ruined fortress.
- Climbing up a tree to gain a vantage point.
- Ascending a cliffside with minimal gear.


## Swim
Used when moving through water or other fluids, Swim reflects both technique and stamina for staying afloat and moving efficiently.

- Swimming across a fast-moving river.
- Diving into a lake to retrieve a lost item.
- Keeping an unconscious ally afloat.
