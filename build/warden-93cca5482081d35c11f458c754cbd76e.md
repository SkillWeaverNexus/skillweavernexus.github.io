# Warden 
![](/img/origins/warden.png)

Wardens are individuals who have taken on the sacred duty of protection through vigilance. Whether guarding ancient sites, protecting communities, or serving as elite guards, they've developed an almost supernatural ability to sense threats before they fully materialize. If you can cast magic, your Arcanic die is Perception. 

### Features 

:::{dropdown} Vigilant Presence
:open:
You cannot be surprised in combat. Once per full rest, when you identify an incoming threat, you can extend this immunity to any ally within 10 feet of you.
::: 

:::{dropdown} Protective Instinct
:open: 
When an ally within 10 feet of you is targeted by an attack, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll and to direct it at yourself instead.
::: 

::{dropdown}Warden's Vigil
:open:
During a rest or when stationary for at least 10 minutes, you can enter a state of heightened awareness. You gain +10 to Vigilance checks made to keep watch for danger while in this state. 
:::

### Warden Speciality

When you choose this origin, you also choose a form of warden from the list below, or develop one with your GM: 

- Boundary Warden: Specializes in guarding territorial borders or magical boundaries. You have and are proficient in a trapping kit, which contains tools to facilitate the setting of trip alarms, animal-style traps, along with more creative uses. Some elements of the trapping kit use mild magical effects, but you do not have to be a spellcaster to use them. 
- Hearth Warden: Specializes in protecting communities and settlements. You gain a +3 to personality checks made to direct and protect communities, and if you can cast magic, know how to cast the "Hearth Circle" spell without expending a skill point to learn it.  
- Relic Warden: Trained to protect specific objects or artifacts of importance. You can form relic bonds with up to three items at a time, via a ten minute ritual. Once bonded, you can sense the location of your bonded objects anywhere on the current plane of existence, and can sense if they are moved or interacted with. If you attempt to form an additional bond, you select on your current bonds to release. Note that relic bonds, while arcane in nature, are not a spell and do not require the arcane initiate ability.

:::{tip} _Backstory Hook_
1. What or who were you first trained to protect? Do you still honor that commitment?
2. Was there ever a time your vigilance failed? What were the consequences?
3. Do you prefer to stand as a visible deterrent, or do you work best from the shadows?
4. Is your heightened awareness innate, trained, or perhaps enhanced by some ritual or event?
5. How do you maintain your focus during long periods of vigilance? Do you have specific meditation techniques or rituals?
6. Do you bear any marks or symbols of your station as a Warden? Is there a formal order or tradition you belong to?
:::
