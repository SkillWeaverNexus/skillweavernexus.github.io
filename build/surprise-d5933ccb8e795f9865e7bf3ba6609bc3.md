

# Surprise

In the case of ambushes or surprising situations, the GM can determine that there is sufficient cause to grant one party the benefit of surprise. In this case, a surprise round takes place at the start of combat. 


- First, Initiative is rolled "as normal" and noted for each party in the combat. 
- During the first turn of the surprise round, one member of the **surprising party** can take their full turn immediately, regardless of the rolled initiative.
- During the {term}`Environmental Turn`, all other surprising party members can use their {term}`reactions <Reaction>`, if desired, to trigger effects, make attacks, or otherwise enact the surprising actions. 
- After the environmental turn, the party with the highest initiative in the initiative order takes their turn (even if it is again the surprising party)
- At the top of the round, initiative fully invokes, and the side with the highest initiative proceeds before the environmental round. Combat from here proceeds as normal.
