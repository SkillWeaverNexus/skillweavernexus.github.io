# Grit 
Grit is a finite player resource that can be used for several purposes. Players have a number of grit points equal to half their player level, rounded up. 

- A grit point can be expended to double the damage of an attack after it is rolled.
- A grit point can be used to add +5 to an attack roll, skill check, or fortitude test.
- A grit point can be used to cast a spell for half its toxicity rating (see below)
- A grit point can be used to make a risk roll, using a d20.
- A grit point can be used when taking a hit that drops you to 0 HP to instead leave you at 1 HP. 
- A grit point can be used to overcome incapacitation to either move half your movement, or interact with an object/creature.


Grit points replenish when you complete a full rest. If you take a half rest, you gain back half your grit points, rounded up.
