# Initiative

Skillweaver does not use a fixed-turn initiative system that involves all individuals rolling. Instead, ``initiative'' is granted per group of allied individuals. Under typical circumstances where neither party is surprised, a nominated member of each party of combatants makes a special, flat d20 roll. 

The parties then act in order based on the outcome of that roll. For 2 parties going head to head, they would alternate back and forth from this point, starting with the winner of the check. For 3+ groups fighting at once, the first action by each group is taken in turn and then continues to cycle. 

The second turn of every round is special, and is called the environmental round. Lair actions or persistent weather effects or collapsing dungeons are all examples of environmental effects that can affect combat, and are resolved during this turn. This turn comes second in each round regardless of initiative, which "skips" this turn.

When it is your group’s turn to act, anyone within your group can choose to take their turn. It is up to you to strategize and decide who has the best move prepared or is setting up for a combination move later. Every individual gets 1 turn per round of combat.
