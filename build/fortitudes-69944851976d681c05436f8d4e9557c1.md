# Fortitudes 

Sometimes, effects in the world will force you to make split second decisions, resist nefarious magic, or dodge incoming fire. Your character's fortitudes determine the outcome of these scenarios. Fortitudes are the combination of two of your base skills. The GM will indicate the DL for these rolls.

There are 5 Fortitudes:

- Psychic Fortitude, governing your resistance to stress, psychic magic, etc., which is your reasoning + personality score
- Physical Fortitude, governing your ability to tough out harmful effects to your body, which is your Physicality+Nature score
- Dexterous Fortitude, governing your ability to quickly react to your surroundings, which is your Physicality + perception score
- Deceptive Fortitude, governing your ability to keep your intentions or desires hidden, which is your stealth + reasoning score, 
- Character Fortitude, governing your ability to not be cowed or intimidated, which is your Physicality + personality score


When you are asked to "Test your [Psychic,etc.] Fortitude", you roll both dice and provide the sum. For example, if your reasoning is a d12 and your personality is a d6, then a test of your psychic fortitude means simply rolling 1d6+1d12. Some scenarios, spells, or items may give you bonuses to this roll, e.g., +1 to all Psychic Fortitude rolls.

Alternatively, when you have an ability that forces another creature or object to make a fortitude test against you, the DL they must beat is your scores (dice max values) added. In the above example, Your DL for forcing a psychic fortitude test is 18.
