# Combat Awareness and Cover

## Combat Awareness
In combat, all sufficiently intelligent creatures have an awareness of where all detectable combatants on the map are, or their last detected location. For creatures relying on sight, this means all visible creatures not behind cover, and an awareness of the last known location of creatures that become invisible or dart behind solid cover. When a creature or object moves 20ft (4 grid spaces) while out of sight of another creature, their current position is considered “unknown” to them, otherwise, their general vicinity is still known.

Only creatures with known location can be directly targeted by actions like attacks or spells; targets with unknown locations can still be impacted by area affects, potentially revealing their position.
