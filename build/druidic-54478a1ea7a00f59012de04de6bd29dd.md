# Druidic
![](img/origins/druidic.png)


You have a close connection to the natural world and your Arcanic die, if you cast spells, is Nature. 

### Features

:::{dropdown} Friendly Contact
:open:
You can wordlessly communicate basic ideas and concepts with plants and animals, and have +2 to Personality checks to encourage animals to obey or help you. 
::: 

:::{dropdown} Affine Types 
:open: 
-  Common Bird (e.g., crow, raven, swallow)
-  Common Rodent (e.g., mouse, rat, hamster)
-  Common Domesticate (e.g., dog, cat, ferret)
-  Common Insect (e.g., fly, spider)


For the cost of 3 TOX, you can turn into a reasonable example of your affine type, and can drop the form at will. The form has 5 HP, and dropping to 0 HP while in this form instantly reverts you to standard form, with damage carrying over. When you reach a player level of 5, you can choose a second affine type, when you reach level 10 you can choose a third, and at level 14, you can select the final type. 
:::
