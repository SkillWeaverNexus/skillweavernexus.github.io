# Magic 

Skillweaver allows for worlds with and without magic, but the expectation is that most settings will have some degree of magic, and SWM provides mechanics and spells to enable such worlds. 

Because of SWN's classless structure, there are no pre-defined silos of magical or non-magical characters. However, there are a few things that are involved with the learning of magic that may be more appealing to some builds: in particular, the ability to learn and cast  spells at all (the ``Arcane Initiate'' ability), is one that is in the Reasoning skill tree, and the ability to learn new spells are also spots in the Reasoning Tree. Thus, any heavily magic build will have at least some strong investment in the Reasoning tree.
