# Courier
![](/img/origins/courier.png)

You ran important missives, materiel, or even contraband across cities and across empires. During your time in this role, you built up an impressive ability to both brave inhospitable terrain, travel long distances without tiring, or, when needed, sprinting to avoid dangers to your charges. 

### Features

:::{dropdown} Practiced Traveler
:open: 
Your base movement speed is increased by 5 feet, and you have a +2 bonus to sprint checks and endurance checks. 
:::

:::{dropdown} Hidden Pockets
:open:
You are well trained in hiding important documents and items on your person. When someone searches your person for something you have previously described as hidden, they have major disadvantage on the investigation check.
:::

:::{dropdown} Courier Network
:open: 
You generally know how messages and important items are moved throughout the region in which you operated, and can tap into this network to learn useful information or to have messages sent rapidly. 
:::

:::{tip} _Backstory Hooks_
1. Who did you carry messages for? Military, merchants, criminals, or someone else?
2. What's the most dangerous delivery you ever made?
3. Did you ever fail to deliver something important? What happened?
4. What's the most valuable thing you've ever transported?
5. Are you still a card-carrying member of your service or guild? If not, what led to your exit? Are you on good terms?
:::
