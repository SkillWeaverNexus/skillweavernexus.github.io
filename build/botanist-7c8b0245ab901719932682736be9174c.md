# Botanist
![](../../img/origins/botanist.png)

You have a special connection to plants. You have a +2 bonus to rolls related to the identification of plants or the creation of extracts from plans for use in decoctions and other elixirs. If you can cast magic, your Arcanic die is Nature.

You have access to a special, rare tome known as the Verdant Codex, whose pages flower with new knowledge when rare plants are pressed between its pages. As you gather new and interesting plants, the codex provides recipes for powerful elixirs. You can learn more about the Verdant Codex, and the cycle it traces, in Chapter N.
