# Personality

Personality governs your ability to interact with others — whether through honesty, manipulation, performance, or sheer force of presence. It is invoked in social situations and acts of charm or control.

## Persuasion
Used to convince others through logical or heartfelt argument, Persuasion is the basis for diplomacy, leadership, and negotiation.

- Inspiring a group to rally to your cause.
- Convincing a noble to back your expedition.
- Calming a hostile crowd.


Deception
Deception is used to obscure truth — lying, feinting, or misleading in both speech and behavior.

- Lying to a guard about your identity.
- Faking an injury to gain sympathy.
- Bluffing your way past a locked checkpoint.


Performance
This skill governs intentional performance — whether as entertainment or distraction — from acting to music to spectacle.

- Playing a song to entertain a tavern.
- Reenacting a battle for a noble court.
- Pretending to be a drunken fool while scouting.

Intimidation
Through threat or sheer presence, Intimidation forces others to consider you dangerous — a tool of pressure and control.

- Forcing a bandit to back down at sword-point.
- Barking orders that freeze enemy morale.
- Using your reputation to silence opposition.


Charm
Charm is the social grease that smooths interaction — winning affection, admiration, or attention without direct persuasion.

- Gaining favor at a royal ball.
- Flirting your way into a guarded compound.
- Making a merchant eager to help you.

To put a point on it: Persuasion is convincing a guard they have a reason to let you in. Charm is them letting you in despite knowing there is no reason --- they just want to impress you. 

Insight
Insight allows you to read the emotional state, hidden motives, or subtle cues of others, particularly in conversations or social settings.

- Sensing that a lord is lying about the treaty.
- Knowing when a friend is hiding something.
- Recognizing discomfort in a hostage.
