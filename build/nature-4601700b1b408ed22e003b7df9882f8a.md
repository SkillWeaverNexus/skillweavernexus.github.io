# Nature 
Nature is the study of the body and mind in both health and affliction. It encompasses both mundane and arcane knowledge about how to treat, manipulate, or understand living systems. While some characters might focus on tending wounds, others may use this skill to create devastating toxins or unravel the causes behind supernatural illness.

## Injury
Understanding and treating physical wounds, or exploiting vulnerable spots to cause them. Also used to stabilize or assess physical condition.

- Determining how serious a wound is, and how it might be treated.
- Applying first aid or stabilizing a bleeding ally.
- Identifying how best to injure or disable a restrained target.


## Disease
Knowledge of communicable or ambient diseases, including magical illnesses. Useful in identifying sources, symptoms, and treatments.

- Diagnosing an unknown plague sweeping through a village.
- Determining if a fever is mundane or magically induced.
- Crafting a biological weapon, or protecting against one.


## Mind
Addresses the mental health and psychic state of individuals. Can also detect or resist magical influence and manipulation.

- Sensing if a character is under charm, compulsion, or magical sleep.
- Aiding an ally in resisting fear or confusion effects.
- Diagnosing long-term psychological trauma or magical obsession.


## Biota 
Understanding of the natural world, particularly flora and fauna and the uses of each. 

- Identifying a plant useful for a healing salve.
- Extracting venom from a slain creature.
- Crafting a blade poison or anti-venom.
- Identifying the cause of death in a poisoned victim.
- Inferring that a forest is sickened by some curse.


## Alchemy
The manipulation of reactive materials to create concoctions, salves, explosives, or augmentations.

- Mixing two known ingredients to make an explosive or firebomb.
- Distilling a healing potion from scavenged reagents.
- Constructing a smoke bomb or oil slick in the field.
