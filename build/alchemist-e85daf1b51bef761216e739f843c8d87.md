# Alchemist
![](/img/origins/alchemist.png)


You have special training and intuition for the alchemical arts. If you can cast magic, your Arcanic die is Reasoning.
### Features 

:::{dropdown} Alchemical Intuition
:open: 
You have a +2 all checks to identify, analyze, or understand alchemical substances, potions, or compounds. When examining an unknown substance, you can often determine its properties with a simple Alchemy check.

:::{dropdown} Efficient Formulation
:open: 
When crafting any alchemical item (potion, poison, bomb, etc.) during downtime, you require half the typical ingredient cost, and lower the project DL by 20. 
:::

:::{dropdown} Alchemist's Kit Proficiency

You start with a specialized kit of glassware, implements, and basic reagents, and are proficient in its use. This kit serves as a portable laboratory, allowing you to attempt certain simple alchemical preparations even without access to a full lab. With this kit, you can Identify common poisons or potions, create one simple alchemical item with 10 uninterrupted minutes, and stabilize volatile alchemical compounds that might otherwise deteriorate with a successful ability check.

:::{tip} _Backstory Hooks_
1. Who taught you the fundamentals of alchemy? Was it formal training or self-discovery?
2. Have you ever created something that had unexpected consequences? What happened?
3. Do you have a signature formula or creation you're known for?
4. What's the rarest or most valuable ingredient you've worked with?
5. Do you view alchemy as a science, an art, or something spiritual?
:::
