
# Craftables

## Engineering/Tinkered Devices

### Grapnel Launcher (Moderate)
Components: 2 Uncommon (spring mechanism, reinforced housing), 1 Common (hook and line)
Effect: Allows firing a grappling hook up to 60 feet with accuracy
Duration: Permanent until damaged


### Smoke Bomb (Simple)

Components: 2 Common (container, fuse), 1 Uncommon (chemical mixture)
Effect: Creates a 15-foot cloud of smoke that provides concealment
Duration: 1d4+1 rounds
Crafting Time: 2 hours during downtime


### Lockbreaker (Moderate)

Components: 2 Uncommon (precision mechanism, reinforced bits), 1 Common (handle)
Effect: Provides +4 to checks to open locks
Duration: Single use (difficult locks) or 1d4 uses (simple locks)
Crafting Time: 4 hours during downtime

### Rebreather (Complex)

Components: 2 Uncommon (filter system, mouthpiece), 1 Rare (air regulation membrane)
Effect: Allows breathing underwater or in toxic environments for 30 minutes
Duration: 3 uses before requiring maintenance
Crafting Time: 8 hours during downtime


### Lightning Capacitor (Complex; Arcane Engineer)

Components: 1 Uncommon (metal casing), 1 Rare (conductive material), 1 Very Rare (energy crystal)
Effect: Stores electrical energy that can be released to deal 2d6 lightning damage
Duration: Single use, rechargeable during downtime
