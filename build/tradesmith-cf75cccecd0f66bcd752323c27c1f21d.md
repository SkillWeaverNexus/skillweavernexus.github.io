You earned your living through physical work - mining, construction, smithing, or other demanding trades that built your body and endurance through daily toil.

:::{dropdown} Practiced Endurance
:open:
You can work twice as long as others before suffering fatigue. During downtime activities requiring physical labor, you complete them in half the normal time.
:::

:::{dropdown} Tradecraft
:open: 
You have proficiency with tools related to your chosen trade. When using these tools for their intended purpose, you have major advantage on relevant checks.
:::

:::{dropdown} Physical Resilience
:open: 
Your hands and body are toughened from years of work, acting as a form of light armor. When fighting without armor, your own body acts as light armor with +5 negation to non-magical blunt and force damage and +3 to other forms. 
:::

:::{tip} _Backstory Hooks_
1. What specific trade did you practice, and for how long?
2. Did you work independently or as part of a guild or crew?
3. What was the most impressive or dangerous project you contributed to?
4. Why did you leave your trade for the adventuring life?
:::
