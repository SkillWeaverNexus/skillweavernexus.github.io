# Reasoning
Reasoning governs the mental acuity of a character — their ability to deduce, recall, understand, and interact with the arcane or logical structure of the world. It is the seat of magic, science, and mental puzzles.

## Logic
The application of structured thinking, Logic governs the ability to solve puzzles, spot logical fallacies, and analyze systems.

- Solving a runic sequence to open a portal.
- Determining how to disable a magical lock.
- Planning a trap with minimal resources.
- Extracting information from tomes and scrolls.


## Arcana
Arcana captures a character's understanding of magic — its flow, form, and behavior — whether learned from books, intuition, or raw experience.

- Identifying an active spell or its resonance.
- Guessing the purpose of a glowing glyph.
- Channeling magical energy into a ritual.


## History
History governs knowledge of past civilizations, major events, legendary figures, and ancient artifacts. GMs can give circumstantial bonuses (typically +1 to +3) for History checks by characters that have strong reason to know the fact at hand. 

- Recalling the details of an old war.
- Identifying a royal crest from a ruined banner.
- Knowing the fall of a forgotten empire.


## Tinkering
Tinkering represents the application of mechanical knowledge to physical objects — understanding how things are built, fixed, or repurposed.

- Constructing a clockwork trap from scavenged parts.
- Disassembling a device to find a hidden compartment.
- Modifying a crossbow to fire underwater.

## Investigation
The act of closely examining something — not just seeing it, but studying it. Investigation is deliberate and careful.

- Reading faded script or deciphering a cipher.
- Studying a crime scene for blood spatter or signs of forced entry.
- Detecting a slight tampering with a lock or device.
- Identifying a tripwire trap in a hallway.

## Religion
Either the academic understanding of religion or faith thereof.

- Knowing a temple's practices and tenets
- Communing with a spiritual entity
- Identifying religious artifacts and their purpose.
