# Arcane Toxicity 

All magic comes with a cost. Channeling great forces through oneself slowly builds up a toxicity that if unchecked, can hurt or even kill you. SWM tracks this toxicity via Arcane Toxicity, or TOX for short. 

Every use of magic in the system (namely, spells, but also magical effects in general) will list an associated TOX score. This number describes both the amount of toxicity it imparts on the caster, but also serves as the spell's `level', capturing something about the intensity or scope of the effect. 

The ability to cast magic is open to any character in SWM, via the special "Arcane Initiate" feat in the Reasoning tree. It is a Tier 4 skill, meaning that one must be willing to expend at least 9 skill points in that tree by minimum allocations to have access to it, but it otherwise has no prerequisites. We will colloquially refer to magic caster's as ``mages'' in this document, but any setting specific names or delinations could be further applied. 

## Threshold Values

The build up of TOX is quantized into integer points, but unlike a spell-slot like system, there is not a hard limit for ``running out'' of the ability to cast arcana. Instead, characters have a so-called Arcanic Threshold Value, or ATV, that describes the amount of TOX they can tolerate before suffering adverse effects. One can cast past this level, but will begin to take on status conditions and other negative consequences for doing so. 

| Player Level | Arcane TOX Value | 
| --- | --- | 
|   	1  | 5 |
|   	2  | 8 |
|   	3  | 10 | 
|        4 | 15 | 
|        5+ | 20 | 


The cap at 20 TOX reflects the typical arcanic limit of a mortal body. Some special items, or capstone abilities, can unlock higher ATV. 

When you choose to push above the ATV of your character to continue casting magic, you begin to take on the effects described by the following table: 
| Amount over ATV | Effect | 
| --- | --- | 
|   	+1  | All spells cost +1 Stamina to cast |
|   	+3  | All spells cost +1 Stamina and deal 2d4 damage to the caster |
|   	+5 |  All spells cost +2 Stamina and deal 2d12 damage to the caster, | 
|        +7 | Imparts Exhaustion, +2 Stamina, 2d12 damage, and d20 roll, if higher than player level, immediate drop to 0 hp | 
|        +9 | +2 stamina, immediate incapacitation


```{note}
At low levels, most spells have a TOX of 1-3. This means that as a 3rd level player (TV 10), you can cast 3-10 spells before suffering adverse effects, depending on which you cast. As always, you can push past these limits, but at your own peril!
```

### Equipment TOX

Martial characters are not left out entirely from thinking about arcane toxicitiy. Magical weapons, even martial ones, tend to carry a low, but non-zero TOX cost. This means that a `use' of the weapon or item increases the TOX of the bearer, and usage beyond their ATV invokes the typical effects, unless stated otherwise on the item. Martial users can, of course, switch to conventional weapons when nearing their ATV, or choose to take additional risk to continue gaining the benefits the arcane weapon provides. 

### Healing Tox
Another critical consideration for toxicity is that being healed via magic increases the recipients' arcane toxicity. Healing spells, Potions imbued with magic, etc., provide the most potent healing effects, but also push you toward your ATV. This might, at certain times, encourage mundane healing techniques which are less potent but do not have this associated cost. 

When you cast a healing spell on yourself, you do not take additional tox beyond the casting cost, representing your innate knowledge of your own body. 

Because toxicity only resets on a full rest, eventually, a party of adventurers will be forced to rest both so that spellcasters clear their tox to cast spells, and so that damage soaking tanks can clear their tox in order to be healable.
