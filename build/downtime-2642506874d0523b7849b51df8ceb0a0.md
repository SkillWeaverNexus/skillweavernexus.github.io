# Rests & Downtime 

## Rest and Recovery

Rest is critical for maintaining health and internal resources. SWN takes a slightly aggressive approach to how rest interacts with in-game time. It defines two forms of rest: 
- **Half Rest**: 8 hours of recovery in the form of sleep or idle/downtime activity, and
- **Full Rest**: 24 hours of recovery in the form of sleep or idle/downtime activity. 

A half rest, as its name indicates, restores half of your deficit in hit points, clears half your current {term}`Arcane Toxicity`, and restores you to half your grit point total, rounded up. A full rest restores all hit points, clears all Arcane Toxicity, and restores grit points to max.

The key distinction is that at the end of a long, standard adventuring day, a simple sleep is not enough to bring you back to maximum

## Downtime 
Downtime is a critical part of adventuring in SWN. Numerous activities can take place during downtime, including learning new spells, building or modifying equipment and armor, research projects with materials obtained during adventures, and more. 

Throughout this book, you'll find examples of activities or tasks that reference that they take place during downtime. We allow GMs latitude in defining just how long your party must ``chill out'' and rest to constitute downtime; we suggest at minimum that 8-12 hours are required for a short downtime, while full days are especially conducive to making downtime progress. Note that Downtime contributes toward Rest and Recovery (next section).

### Projects 

Most things that happen during downtime are characterized as Projects. Projects have a specific DL to achieve them that is generally much higher than for a single action. When you make a project roll during downtime, it represents your extended effort, and you keep track of a total tally. Once you reach that final DL, your project is complete. 

Depending on the length of a given downtime, you may be able to make a more than one project roll, which you can either split between multiple projects or apply toward the same one. As a general guideline, a full day's work (12 hours) is the amount of time covered by 1 project roll. Some abilities and origins indicate you can carry out certain projects more efficiently. For example, an Engineer (origin ability) can build certain devices in half the normal time --- this means that they can make 2 checks per day toward their tinkering project, rather than one. It is possible of course to ``stay up all night working,'' but rolls made after a point will incur disadvantage from lack of sleep. 

The primary axes for projects are

- Time: related to the total DL, defines how many checks will likely be needed to complete the project.
- Materials: might need to be gathered or sought out. As described in Chapter N, materials generally have rarity tags which indicate whether they are readily available during downtime, or whether they must be obtained more specifically, e.g., while adventuring, or outside of downtime.
