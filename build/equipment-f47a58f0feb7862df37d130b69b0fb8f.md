# Equipment

Equipment and kits are an important part of many builds. We've already discussed combat equipment; the table is reproduced here for completeness: 


| Tree | Category | Examples | 
| --- | --- | --- |
|Stealth	 | Light Weapons | Daggers, light crossbows, throwing knives|
|Physicality	 |Medium, Heavy Martial Weapons  |Shortswords, Longswords, Great Swords, Pikes/Halberds, heavy crossbows|
|Nature | Concoctions | Poisons, oils, alchemical bombs, potions |
|Perception | Ranged Weapons | Medium crossbows, heavy crossbows, long bows |
|Personality | Finesse Weapons | Rapiers, Dual Swords, Whip, Lasso | 
|Reasoning | Magic-tuned Weapons | Staffs, Wands, Orbs, Artifice |

The Tree listed indicates that the abilities needed to gain proficiency, expertise, and mastery in each weapon category are found in that tree. 

Things in SWN that have the `equipment` tag require proficiency to be used without the standard -10 penalty. 

```{note}
This is in contrast to the `item` tag, which generally does not require proficiency for use.
```

## Kits 

There are several kits available in the game. These are considered relatively mundane, potentially expensive, but not rare, and can be reasonably obtained if you do not start with one. The defined kits, which are `equipment`, are

| --- | ---- | ----------- |

Below are more detailed descriptions of each kit. 

### Forgery Kit (Personality)

A comprehensive collection of tools for creating convincing forgeries and falsifications. Contains various papers and parchments of different qualities, inks in multiple colors and compositions, seal stamps and wax of various types, tools for precise copying of handwriting and signatures, reference samples of common official documents, materials for aging documents artificially, and equipment for examining and authenticating existing documents. Also contains clay-mould and wax, along with metallic paints, allowing for reasonable forgeries to be made of rings, baubles, and other small items or import. 

Allows you to 
- Create convincing false documents (identification papers, permits, letters of introduction)
- Forge signatures and seals of officials or other important individuals
- Detect forgeries created by others through careful examination
- Duplicate keys from wax impressions
- Create counterfeit currency or valuable certificates
- Open and reseal documents without detection
- Age or weather documents to match established timelines
- Create false credentials or authorizations

When using this kit, you 
- Have minor advantage on Personality/Deception checks when using forged documents in tandem.
- can identify other forgeries or authenticate other documents and items
- can tamper with existing documents without obvious signs, e.g., recreating a wax seal.

When you use your forgery kit to forge something, the GM will set a DL for the challenge associated with the document. If you are attempting this during {term}`Downtime`, you can take your time to work carefully, and are guaranteed to hit the DL with enough time. For any more hasty attempts, the quality of the forgery will depend on only one or two rolls.
