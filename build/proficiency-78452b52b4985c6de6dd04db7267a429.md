# Proficiency, Expertise, and Mastery

SWN defines three levels of ability with respect to most `equipment`, which are generally earned as abilities. 

- **Proficiency** is a prequisite for the use of most equipment, otherwise a -10 penalty is imposed on ability rolls made with that equipment.
- **Expertise** requires proficiency and is higher up the tree, granting bonuses to rolls made with that equipment. 
- **Mastery** is the pinnacle of use for a piece of equipment or weapon type, and grants several boons.
