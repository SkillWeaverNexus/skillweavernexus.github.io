# Combat Awareness and Cover

## Combat Awareness
In combat, all sufficiently intelligent creatures have an awareness of where all detectable combatants on the map are, or their last detected location. For creatures relying on sight, this means all visible creatures not behind cover, and an awareness of the last known location of creatures that become invisible or dart behind solid cover. When a creature or object moves 20ft (4 grid spaces) while out of sight to enemy combatants, their position becomes unknown. 

Only creatures with known location can be directly targeted by actions like attacks or spells; targets with unknown locations can still be impacted by area affects, potentially revealing their position. 

When a creature whose position is unknown becomes revealed, either by becoming visible, moving into line of sight, or making an attack, they gain the Protected status until they move 10 ft or end their turn. This status prevents them from being targeted by reactions. 

:::{note} On Invisibility
Unless otherwise noted, spells cast from invisibility still reveal the location of the caster, and ranged projectiles (e.g., arrows, throwing stars) become visible when they leave the invisible individual's vicinity. In both cases, such attacks reveal the location of the attacker, even if they are otherwise hidden or invisible.
:::

## Cover 



:::{note} Example

Your party is fighting in a temple, and an enemy archer is hiding in the rafters above, taking pot shots. You see them duck down and out of site, defining your last known position of them. 

If they pop up 20 ft down the rafters, you don't have time to both clock their appearance and line up a reactionary shot/cast; they use their grace period to take a shot and duck down.
