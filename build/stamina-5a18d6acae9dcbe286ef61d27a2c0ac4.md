# Stamina
At the core of the SWN combat system is stamina. Stamina is a renewable resource that all characters have. Nearly all of the time, characters have 6 stamina per round, resetting at the start of the player's turn. 

```{note}
While the 6-stamina-per-turn maxim will serve for much of the game, some capstone abilities provide characters with additional stamina, and others provide temporary recovery of stamina during a round.
```


Stamina fuels nearly everything you do in combat, defining how you choose to allocate the limited time and energy in the few seconds that constitute a round. 

- Attacks (both martial and arcane) require stamina to carry out 
- Defense, including parrying and dodging, require stamina
- Reactions, which are taken at will during combat, require stamina
- Some abilities specifically denote a stamina cost
