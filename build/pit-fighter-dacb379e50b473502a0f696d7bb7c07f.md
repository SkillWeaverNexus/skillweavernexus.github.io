# Pit Fighter 

![](/img/origins/pit-fighter.png)
You fought for entertainment in arenas, pits, or underground fighting circuits, learning to survive and thrive in close-quarters combat while playing to the crowd.
### Features

:::{dropdown} Crowd Favorite
:open:
When fighting in front of spectators, you gain +2 to attack and damage rolls. The GM determines when spectators are present.
:::

:::{dropdown} Unarmed Expertise
:open:
You fight best with fists and feet. When fighting unarmed (excluding gloves and gauntlets), you can make two strikes for every one stamina spent. 
:::

:::{dropdown} Dirty Fighting
:open:
Once per combat when you make an unarmed strike, you can add your Physicality die value to the damage on a successful hit, representing a particularly brutal or unsporting technique.
:::

:::{tip} _Backstory Hook_
1. Were you a voluntary fighter or forced into the pits?
2. Did you have a fighting persona or stage name?
3. What was your most memorable victory or defeat?
4. Why did you leave the fighting circuit?
:::
