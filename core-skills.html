Skills and Subskills

Here we describe the core skills and their circumstantial or sub skills in more detail, and provide examples for DMs and Players to understand how general ability checks work in the game.

As a reminder, the core skills are

  • Physicality, which covers (in general) both strength and dexterity based abilities,
  • Nature, which covers health, including mundane and magical healing, poisons and decoctions, and alchemical development,
  • Personality, which covers social abilities, including persuasion and deception,
  • Perception, which covers degrees of noticing and detecting with the various senses,
  • Reasoning, which covers logic and insight and the use of magic, and
  • Stealth, which covers all aspects of avoiding detection.
