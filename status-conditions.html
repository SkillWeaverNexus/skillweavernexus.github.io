Status Conditions

SWN has several status conditions that can apply to a creature or in some cases non-creature. These involve states of bleeding, burning, freezing, etc., and also capture some key states involved with being dropped to 0 HP or otherwise heavily reduced in capacity.

ConditionEffect
BleedingA bleeding entity has a wound that deals damage over time. At the start of their turn, they automatically take 1 die of bleed damage (depending on the effect) and loose 2 stamina. Bleeding can be staunched via 4 stamina (active staunching), by any magical healing effect, or by specialty potions designed specifically to stop bleeding.
BurningA burning entity takes a die of fire damage at the start of their turn and looses 2 stamina. Burning can be put out as a 2 stamina action (stop drop and roll), via any magical healing, potions, or if present, by submerging or dousing with water or other non-flammable liquid.
CharmedA charmed entity is drained of an amount of vitality over time. At the start of their turn, they automatically take 1 die of psychic damage and loose 2 stamina. Additionally, charmed creatures cannot attack or harm the creature that charmed it, and treats this creature as an ally. At the end of a charmed creatures’ turn, it makes a Psychic Fortitude test to break the charm.\
DyingUnconscious and making risk/death rolls during the environmental turn.
FreezingA freezing entity takes the relevant die of cold damage at the start of their turn and looses 2 stamina. The freezing condition can be broken via potions, any healing magic, and certain spells, or by leaving the region causing the active condition.
IncapacitatedYou cannot move, interact, make attack actions, or channel spells in combat, unless a grit is used to facilitate the first two. You have 0 HP and have no stamina, but can make simple perception, personality, or reasoning checks. Out of combat, you can move with some assistance. Achieving a rest while incapacitated changes your condition to diminished.
DiminishedYou have a max HP of 20 and 3 stamina/round. All attacks are made with -5 penalty, and your toxicity is at minimum half its TV, and your maximum movement is halved. Resting while diminished changes your condition to exhausted, and healing spells cannot heal you for more than those 20 HP.\
ExhaustedYou have a -2 penalty to all attacks. If improving to exhausted from diminished, all other effects (changes to HP, stamina, movement) are removed.
FrightenedChecks made via personality and reasoning have a -2 penalty.
GrappledA grappled entity cannot move, parry, or dodge. Grappled entities can break a grapple on their turn using 4 stamina.
PoisonedA poisoned creature takes a die of poison damage at the start of their turn, and they loose 2 stamina. The condition can be lifted via potions or any magical healing.
ProneYou are lying on the ground. You cannot parry or dodge. Standing up costs 3 stamina.
BloodiedYou have less than 10 HP; movement is halved, attacks/ability checks are at major disadvantage, and spells have double toxicity cost.
